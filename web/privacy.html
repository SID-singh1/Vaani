Privacy

Vaani turns voice notes into summaries and action items. This page explains exactly what happens to what you send. Last updated: October 2026.

What we store

  • Audio: kept only while it's being processed, then deleted. We never store your recordings.
  • Transcripts, summaries and action items: stored so you can see your history. You can delete any note, or all of them, at any time (web: the trash icon; Telegram: /delete; WhatsApp: reply delete all). Deletion is permanent.
  • Who you are: on the web, a random ID kept in your browser (no account, no email). On Telegram, your numeric Telegram user ID. On WhatsApp, a one-way keyed hash of your phone number. The number itself is never stored.
  • Ratings and feedback you choose to send.

Who processes your audio

Fast mode (the default on the hosted service) sends audio to Groq for speech-to-text, and the transcript to Google's Gemini API for the summary (with Groq as a backup when Gemini is unavailable). The hosted service uses Gemini's free tier. Under Google's terms for unpaid use, Google may use submitted content to improve its products, and human reviewers may read it. Please don't send anything you wouldn't want a third party to see.

Private mode, where a server offers it, runs Whisper and a local language model on that server's own CPU. Nothing is sent to any AI company. It's slower. Vaani is open source, so you can self-host it and keep everything on your own machine.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no selling or sharing your data, and no training models on your notes.
  • No tracking cookies or analytics scripts on this site.

Usage numbers

The developer sees aggregate statistics (number of notes, active users, processing times) and masked IDs in an admin dashboard, to keep the service working and improve it.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/SID-singh1/Vaani, or use the feedback command in the bot.
